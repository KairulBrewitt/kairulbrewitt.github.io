Quasi-static model of a 1/10 4WD touring car, set up as an RC Maker SP2 by default. The suspension geometry is solved in full 3D for every setting. Cornering and acceleration are treated as steady state, with no damper, tyre-slip or inertia dynamics. Each axle's lateral force follows the CG position and is shared between its two tyres in proportion to load.

SP2 values marked KIT come from RC Maker’s manual and product pages. Part positions RC Maker does not publish are estimates (EST), with the steering calibrated to the kit’s published lock.
